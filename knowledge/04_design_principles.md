# Design Principles

## Design Philosophy

Design exists to solve business problems.

Every design decision must support usability, clarity and measurable outcomes.

A beautiful interface without business value is incomplete.

---

## Core Principles

- Business goals drive design.
- Users come before stakeholders.
- Simplicity over complexity.
- Clarity over decoration.
- Consistency over novelty.
- Accessibility is mandatory.
- Performance is part of UX.
- Every interaction should have a purpose.

---

## Visual Language

Modern.

Minimal.

Premium.

Confident.

Enterprise.

Timeless.

Never trendy.

Never overdesigned.

---

## Layout Principles

Prefer generous whitespace.

Use strong visual hierarchy.

Keep layouts modular.

Design responsive by default.

Use predictable patterns.

Reduce cognitive load.

---

## Typography

Typography is the primary design tool.

Use size, weight and spacing before using color.

Never rely on decoration.

Readable first.

---

## Components

Reuse components whenever possible.

Avoid creating one-off sections.

Every component must be reusable.

Prefer composition over duplication.

---

## Motion

Motion supports understanding.

Never animate for decoration.

Animations should feel smooth, subtle and purposeful.

---

## UX

Reduce friction.

One primary action per section.

Clear navigation.

Fast scanning.

Accessible interactions.

---

## AI Instructions

Never generate generic agency layouts.

Prefer reusable components.

Respect the design system.

Never introduce visual complexity without justification.

Every design decision should support business goals.
