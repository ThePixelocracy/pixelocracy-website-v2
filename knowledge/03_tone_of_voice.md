# Tone of Voice

## Purpose

This document defines how Pixelocracy communicates across the website, landing pages, blog articles, case studies and AI-generated content.

---

## Voice

Professional.

Confident.

Clear.

Practical.

Human.

Thoughtful.

Modern.

Credible.

---

## We write...

- With clarity before cleverness.
- Using simple language for complex ideas.
- Focusing on business value.
- Like experienced practitioners.
- With confidence, never arrogance.
- Short, readable paragraphs.
- Active voice.
- Direct language.

---

## We don't write...

- Marketing fluff.
- Buzzwords without meaning.
- Corporate jargon.
- Empty claims.
- Clickbait.
- Hype.
- Fake urgency.
- Generic agency language.

---

## Preferred Style

Explain.

Educate.

Guide.

Support every statement with reasoning.

Focus on outcomes.

Prefer examples over adjectives.

---

## Headlines

Short.

Clear.

Benefit driven.

Business focused.

Never vague.

---

## Calls To Action

Confident.

Helpful.

Action-oriented.

Never pushy.

Examples:

- Let's talk.
- Explore the possibilities.
- Build something better.
- Start the conversation.
- See how we can help.

---

## AI Instructions

Never invent facts.

Never exaggerate.

Never use statistics unless approved.

Never change approved messaging.

When uncertain, ask.

Maintain one consistent voice across the entire website.
