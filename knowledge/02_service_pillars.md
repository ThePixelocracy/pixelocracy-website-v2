## 1. Strategy & Advisory

### Overview

We decode the transformation imperative. Organizations often have strategy but execution can be challenging. We diagnose, align leadership, and define the roadmap.

### What's Included

- Audits, Fitness Assessment & Diagnostics
- Business Case Development & Financial Modeling
- Journeys, Roadmaps, Solutions Mapping & Sequencing
- Vendor and Solutions Scouting
- RFP Management, Vendor Selection and Delivery Oversight
- Research, Go-to-Market & Positioning Strategy
- Stakeholder Alignment & Change Leadership

### Value Propositions

- Strategy that moves to execution. Not consultant decks. Roadmaps that become operations.
- Business-first thinking. We start with the KPI, then engineer backwards.
- Cross-sector credibility across government, mobility, energy, healthcare, telco, financial services, FMCG and enterprise organizations.

### Client Pains

- "We spent €100K on consultants. The deck is beautiful. Nothing changed."
- "Our transformation plan exists in 47 PowerPoints. Which one is real?"
- "We don't know whether we need a vendor, partner or internal team."
- "Time-to-market is critical and we need more execution velocity."

### Proof Points

- National digital transformation solution supporting more than 1 billion transactions.
- National mobility transformation supporting millions of yearly transactions.
- Large-scale business transformation supporting the transition from traditional sales to digital operations.

### Key Differentiator

From insight to execution. We embed with client teams and stay accountable until strategy becomes reality.

- "Every dashboard tells a different story."

### Key Differentiator

Decision intelligence, not just analytics. We don't simply show the data. We automate what the data tells you to do.
